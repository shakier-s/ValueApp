### Description
ValueApp connects shoppers with money-saving offers from nearby stores, restaurants, cafés and quick shops.

Discover buy-one-get-one-free deals, percentage discounts and fixed-value savings. Save vouchers directly to your phone—nothing to print—and redeem them with a store attendant when you visit.

For shoppers:
- Discover offers from nearby businesses
- Search by store, deal or category
- Save multiple vouchers to your phone
- See expiry dates and remaining availability
- Redeem securely with store-attendant verification
- Keep a clear record of used vouchers

For shop owners:
- Create and publish deals in minutes
- Choose BOGO, percentage or fixed-amount discounts
- Set voucher limits and expiry dates
- Pause or reactivate an offer at any time
- Track redemptions and remaining voucher inventory

ValueApp makes local savings simple for customers and measurable for businesses.

## App Review notes

ValueApp supports two roles in one app. Use the storefront/profile switch button to change between Shopper and Shop Owner modes.

Shopper test flow:
1. Open any deal from Discover.
2. Tap Save voucher.
3. Open My Vouchers and select the saved voucher.
4. Tap Redeem with store attendant.
5. Enter any four-digit code (for example 1234) to complete the test redemption.

Shop owner test flow:
1. Tap the storefront switch button and select Manage deals.
2. Open Create, complete the deal fields, and publish.
3. The new offer appears on the Dashboard and in shopper Discover mode.

The current prototype stores sample and user-created deals locally on-device and does not require sign-in.
